Hand-written imitations of the 2020 Census ZCTA relationship files (#159), in their header layout. They are hand-written, with a few real GEOIDs for the judged cases (for example Richmond city, VA 51760, Richmond County 51159, Henrico 51087 and Hanover 51085, Glen Allen 5131200, Jefferson County, KY 21111) and made-up GEOIDs and names elsewhere; their areas are invented. They are not a copy of any download. tests/test_census_places.py builds a table from them to test tools/census_places.py's rules.
